I began trading the Indian markets in late 2023, with a sharp focus on the index derivatives segment — primarily Nifty and Sensex options and futures. My edge lies in identifying high-probability setups while keeping risk precisely defined on every single trade.

Trading is my primary activity. The majority of capital is deployed into active F&O positions — both intraday and positional — with a small reserve directed into long-term investments through mutual funds, building a parallel layer of compounding wealth alongside the trading book.

Beyond my own book, I run Zinvest — a mutual fund distribution practice helping clients build disciplined, goal-aligned portfolios. Whether you are looking for structured long-term wealth creation or a deeper understanding of the markets, I can help.
