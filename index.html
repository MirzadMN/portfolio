I began trading the Indian markets in late 2023, with a sharp focus on the index derivatives segment — primarily Nifty and BankNifty options and futures. My edge lies in identifying high-probability setups while keeping risk precisely defined on every trade.

Trading is my primary activity. I allocate the majority of capital to active F&O positions — intraday and positional — with a small reserve directed into long-term investments through mutual funds, building a parallel layer of compounding wealth alongside the trading book.

Beyond my own book, I run Zinvest — a mutual fund distribution practice helping others build disciplined, goal-aligned investment portfolios. Whether you're looking for trading insights or structured long-term wealth creation, I can help.
